Agriculture is the backbone of India's economy

All Crops Rabi (Winter) Kharif (Monsoon) Annual

Wheat Rabi

Growing Season: November to April

Major States: Uttar Pradesh, Punjab, Haryana

Facts: India is the 2nd largest producer of wheat globally. It's a staple food in North India.

Rice Kharif

Growing Season: June to November

Major States: West Bengal, Punjab, Uttar Pradesh

Facts: Staple food for 65% of India's population. India is the world's largest exporter of rice.

Maize Kharif

Growing Season: June to September

Major States: Karnataka, Madhya Pradesh, Bihar

Facts: Third most important food crop after rice and wheat. Used for animal feed and industrial products.

Cotton Kharif

Growing Season: April to September

Major States: Gujarat, Maharashtra, Telangana

Facts: India is the world's largest producer of cotton. Known as "White Gold".

Sugarcane Annual

Growing Season: 12-18 month cycle

Major States: Maharashtra, Uttar Pradesh, Karnataka

Facts: India is the 2nd largest producer. Basis for sugar and jaggery industry.

Millets Kharif Rabi

Growing Season: June-November or November-March

Major States: Rajasthan, Karnataka, Maharashtra

Facts: Include Bajra, Jowar, Ragi. Highly nutritious and drought-resistant.

Pulses Rabi

Growing Season: October to March

Major States: Madhya Pradesh, Rajasthan, Maharashtra

Facts: Include chickpeas, lentils, peas. India is the largest producer and consumer.

Tea Annual

Growing Season: Year-round with flushes

Major States: Assam, West Bengal, Tamil Nadu

Facts: India is the 2nd largest tea producer. Assam tea is world famous.

Coffee Annual

Growing Season: Year-round

Major States: Karnataka, Kerala, Tamil Nadu

Facts: India produces both Arabica and Robusta varieties. Grown in Western Ghats.

Mustard Rabi

Growing Season: October to March

Major States: Rajasthan, Haryana, Madhya Pradesh

Facts: Primary source of edible oil in North India. Bright yellow flowers are iconic.
